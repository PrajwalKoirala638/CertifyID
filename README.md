## What is CertifyID?

CertifyID is an identity verification service. Businesses use it to check a person's ID and confirm the information is real and true before trusting them, serving them, hiring them, or letting them in.

In simple words: **someone shows an ID, and CertifyID checks if it's real and if the details match the person.**

## Why this matters

Every day, businesses have to trust that people are telling the truth about who they are — at a register, on a website, at an event, at a door, or during onboarding. Without a reliable way to check, it's just guesswork. CertifyID removes the guesswork.

- **For businesses:** Know for sure who you're dealing with, and reduce exposure to fraud.
- **For customers:** A fair, fast, consistent check — the same process for everyone, no exceptions.
- **For everyone else:** Fewer scams, less identity fraud, more trust in everyday transactions.

## How it works

1. **A person presents their ID.** This could be a driver's license, passport, national ID card, or other government-issued document.
2. **CertifyID checks the document.** The system evaluates whether the document is genuine, currently valid, and unaltered.
3. **CertifyID confirms the person matches the ID.** A live photo or selfie check can confirm the person holding the ID is the person pictured on it.
4. **CertifyID cross-checks the details.** Name, date of birth, and document number are checked for internal consistency and, where applicable, against authoritative records.
5. **You get a clear answer.** Verified, not verified, or needs manual review. No guesswork, no inconsistent judgment calls between staff members.

A verification typically completes in a few seconds to a couple of minutes, depending on the verification level requested.

## What gets checked

| Check | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Document authenticity** | Confirms the ID itself is genuine and not fabricated or altered |
| **Document validity** | Confirms the ID is currently valid and not expired |
| **Face match** | Confirms the person presenting the ID matches the photo on it |
| **Data consistency** | Confirms name, date of birth, and ID number are internally consistent |
| **Liveness check** | Confirms a real, live person is present (not a photo of a photo, a mask, or a screen) |
| **Duplicate/reuse detection** | Flags if the same document or face has already been used elsewhere in a way that looks suspicious |

## Discounts & other benefits

A common reason businesses use CertifyID is to confirm someone qualifies for something before granting it — a discount, a membership rate, early access, or another benefit tied to who a person is.

Instead of a business collecting and holding onto sensitive personal records itself, CertifyID does the check and returns a simple result: **eligible** or **not eligible**. The business never has to see or store the underlying personal details, and the person never has to hand over more information than necessary.

This model is already standard practice at large retailers, and it's the same approach CertifyID follows:

- **Military & veteran discounts** — confirming current or former service status
- **Teacher & student discounts** — confirming enrollment or employment at a school
- **Healthcare & first-responder discounts** — confirming employment in a qualifying role
- **Senior discounts** — confirming age eligibility
- **Membership & loyalty benefits** — confirming someone qualifies for a tier, rate, or perk
- **Age-restricted access** — confirming someone meets a minimum age requirement

**Why this is better than the alternatives businesses use today:**

- **Better than the honor system** — no more taking someone's word for it, which is unfair to customers who actually qualify.
- **Better than manual ID checks** — no more staff eyeballing a card and guessing whether it's real.
- **Better than businesses building their own database** — CertifyID verifies and returns an answer; it doesn't hand businesses a copy of anyone's personal records to hold onto, secure, and eventually have to explain to a regulator.

The goal is a system that works for everyone in the exchange: businesses can offer discounts and benefits with confidence they're going to the right people, and customers get a fast, private, consistent process instead of an inconsistent one that varies by which employee is working the register.

## Verification levels

Not every situation needs the same depth of check. CertifyID offers tiered verification so businesses can match the level of scrutiny to the level of risk:

- **Basic** — document authenticity and validity check only.
- **Standard** — adds a face match between the ID photo and a live selfie.
- **Enhanced** — adds liveness detection and cross-checks against additional records.
- **Custom** — configurable rules for businesses with specific regulatory or risk requirements.

## Accepted ID types

- Driver's licenses
- Passports
- National ID cards
- State or government-issued ID cards
- Residency and immigration documents (where applicable and legally permitted)

Supported document types vary by country and are subject to what can be reliably verified.

## Data & privacy

- CertifyID collects only the information needed to complete a verification.
- Verification results are typically shared as a simple outcome (verified / not verified), not the underlying personal data, unless a business has a specific and lawful reason to receive more.
- Data handling follows applicable privacy law in the regions where CertifyID operates (for example, data protection and consumer privacy regulations vary by country and state).
- People being verified should be clearly informed that a check is happening and what it involves, consistent with applicable consent requirements.

## Compliance & legal

Identity verification touches regulated areas, and any real-world implementation of a service like this needs to account for:

- **Data protection law** in the relevant jurisdiction(s) — what can be collected, how long it can be kept, and how it must be secured.
- **Consent requirements** — many jurisdictions require clear, informed consent before biometric or document data is processed.
- **Anti-discrimination requirements** — verification processes must be applied consistently and cannot be used to unfairly screen out protected groups.
- **Sector-specific rules** — industries like financial services have additional identity-verification obligations (e.g., KYC/AML requirements).
- **Retention limits** — personal and biometric data should not be kept longer than necessary for the stated purpose.

This section is a general orientation, not legal advice — a real deployment should be reviewed by legal counsel familiar with the relevant jurisdictions.

## What CertifyID is not

- CertifyID is **not** a government agency and is **not** affiliated with any government, state, or federal ID system.
- CertifyID does **not** store more personal information than necessary, and does not sell personal data.
- CertifyID does **not** replace a business's own judgment or legal obligations — it is a tool to support decision-making, not the final word in every situation.
- CertifyID is **not** a substitute for a licensed compliance program in regulated industries.

## FAQ

**Does CertifyID store my ID after verification?**
Only for as long as necessary and consistent with applicable law and the business's own retention policy — not indefinitely by default.

**What happens if verification fails?**
The business receives a "not verified" or "needs review" result and decides next steps according to their own policy. CertifyID doesn't make the final decision on the business's behalf.

**Can CertifyID verify people outside the country it operates in?**
Coverage depends on which document types and countries are supported at a given time.

**Is this the same as a background check?**
No. Identity verification confirms someone is who they claim to be. A background check looks into someone's history (criminal record, credit, employment, etc.) and is a separate, more heavily regulated process.
